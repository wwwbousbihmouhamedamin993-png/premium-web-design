# Worked references

Finished screens by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)), in `refs/` next to this file. Look at them before the Visuals pass of a remediation run. Take their qualities — composition, light, restraint, where the moment is placed — never their subject, palette or layout, so every product still gets its own screen. Adapted from [unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop), CC BY 4.0. (`unslop-dashboard-after` is the finished version of the upstream example dashboard, so find your own answer rather than rebuilding it. The upstream fifth example belongs to the story-video feature, which is not ported here.)

**Upstream pin:** ported from heydamir/unslop at commit `4652254daff586ff4739849395a127b56f7f8cc7` (2026-10-29). Re-sync by copying updated upstream files over this folder and keeping the preambles, this pin, and the credit line.

| File | What it shows | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [refs/unslop-hero-after.webp](refs/unslop-hero-after.webp) | An invoicing hero with a paper plane drawn in digits and currency signs, small notes pinned to it, and the product rising from below | The visual is made of the product's own material, money, so it is the moment and the signature at once. Text sits on calm sky, the chrome is quiet, and the product view invites a scroll |
| [refs/unslop-dashboard-after.webp](refs/unslop-dashboard-after.webp) | A finance dashboard with two rendered payment cards and a revenue chart drawn as soft colored curves over a mirrored grey comparison | Quiet graphite chrome, and all the color lives in two moments, a real looking object and a chart where every color means a series |
| [refs/beacon-engraving.webp](refs/beacon-engraving.webp) | An incident monitoring hero drawn as one blue engraving of a lighthouse on a rocky coast, with a serif headline in the same ink | A print style working because the whole page is in it. One ink, one paper tone, and the headline sits in the calm sky the drawing leaves for it |
| [refs/meadow-3d.webp](refs/meadow-3d.webp) | A footer as a soft 3D meadow with a few matte crystal shapes and a huge wordmark standing behind the hills | Friendly stylized 3D with a restrained palette, type that becomes part of the scene, and quiet links on the open sky |

What they share: one bright element per screen, quiet chrome around it, a visual that can be explained in one sentence, and headlines placed on space that was left empty for them.
